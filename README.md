# Lawrence Public Schools — Enrollment Analysis (2019–2024)

15-minute stakeholder presentation analyzing district enrollment trends for
**Lawrence Public Schools (LPS)**, prepared April 2025 for an HR Information
Systems & Data Analyst interview. Built from Massachusetts DESE public
enrollment profiles with Python/matplotlib visualizations.

> Portfolio evidence — an interview work product, not a placement claim.
> The role was not started — I was ghosted after receiving a written job offer.

## Headline findings (from the deck's data tables)

- **Enrollment declined ~3.16% over five years** — 13,748 (2019) → 13,313 (2024),
  a loss of ~435 students, tracking the statewide decline.
- **94.6% Hispanic/Latino enrollment**, far above the 25.9% state average.
- **Gender:** 46.8% female, 53.0% male, 0.1% nonbinary (2024).
- **English learners:** 48.1% of kindergarteners are ELLs; ~85% of ELL students
  speak Spanish at home.
- **Students with disabilities:** 61.2% of Pre-K, 20.0% of kindergarten.
- **Low income:** 86.9% of kindergarteners — Title I funding implications.

## Recommendations delivered

1. **HR** — prioritize bilingual kindergarten teachers, counselors, and autism
   specialists as ELL/SPED shares grow.
2. **DEIB** — expand bilingual programs (e.g., UNIDOS), culturally responsive
   teaching, equitable access reviews.
3. **Technology** — build a real-time enrollment dashboard shared by schools,
   HR, and planning so staffing tracks demographics.

  3.16% decline). The **tables** are the record; notes are flagged as draft.
- A 2024–25 early-grade chart in the working notes was explicitly labeled
  hypothetical — DESE had not published that year in-session.
- Always re-pull current figures from
